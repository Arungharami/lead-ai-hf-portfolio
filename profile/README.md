## What I Build

I design and deploy **production-ready AI systems** for businesses that need fraud detection, customer intelligence, and predictive analytics — with full explainability built in so your team can trust, audit, and act on every prediction.

Every model I build answers three questions a business actually cares about:
- **What is the risk / prediction?** — a clear, actionable output
- **Why did the model decide that?** — a human-readable SHAP explanation
- **What should we do about it?** — decision-support logic, not just a number
